# Corpus Expansion Guide

Add fixtures in this order:

1. Real NIDUS teacher-uploaded PDFs with permission.
2. NDA and CDS mathematics papers with formulas and diagrams.
3. JEE and NEET STEM papers with high visual density.
4. Scanned and rotated papers.
5. DOCX and Office Math papers.
6. Answer-key-only documents.
7. Worked solution documents.
8. Legacy imports already used by teachers.

Every new fixture must include a manifest, original document, all snapshots, and a benchmark threshold review.
