(A) 1/3

Q2. The direction ratios of a line are proportional to (4, -3, 12). The corresponding direction cosines are:
(A) (4/13, -3/13, 12/13)
(B) (4/12, -3/12, 12/12)
(C) (3/13, -4/13, 12/13)
(D) (4/13, 3/13, -12/13)

Answer Key: 1-D, 2-A
